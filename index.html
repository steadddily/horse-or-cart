How the race works
  • Horse: walking pace, 1.4 m/s (Rose's ruling). It loves you, so it never throws you. About 12 minutes per km.
  • Cart: 13.4 m/s, about 30 mph. The street throws 20 hazards per km at it (curbs, potholes, parked cars, turns). Steering is the share you dodge. Every miss is a crash that costs 45 seconds to collect yourself and the cart.
  • Each crash has a 15% chance of a concussion. Go over your spare concussions and the trip ends at urgent care. The horse always arrives.
  • Crashes follow a Poisson process, so the charts are exact, not sampled. The race above is one random draw.
  • Rider settings are guesses from the thread. Nobody has ridden either yet.
